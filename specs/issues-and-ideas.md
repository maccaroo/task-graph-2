# Issues and Ideas
A place for open questions, known issues, and ideas not yet ready to spec.  When an item matures into a requirement, move it to the appropriate spec document and add tasks to `tasks.md`.

---

## Ideas

- **Task Templates** — create a task from a predefined template; repeating tasks become a special case of a template that auto-instantiates
- **"What If" Mode** — temporarily shift a task and preview cascading effects on dependents in the graph, without committing the change
