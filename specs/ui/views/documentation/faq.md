# FAQ View

A static view of frequently asked questions grouped by category.

## Route
`/docs/faq`

## Layout
Rendered inside AppShell. No sidebar.
Questions use an accordion (expand/collapse) pattern — one item open at a time.

## Categories & Questions

### General
- What is TaskGraph?
- Who is TaskGraph for?

### Tasks
- How do I create a task?
- What do the task colours mean?
- What is the difference between Fixed, Flexible, and None for start/end dates?

### Dependencies
- What is a predecessor/successor relationship?
- How do I add a dependency between tasks?
- What relationship types are available?

### Graph View
- How do I navigate the graph (pan/zoom)?
- How do I zoom using the toolbar controls?
- What is the mini-map?
- How do I reposition a task?
- How do I adjust a task's dates by dragging?
- What is the current-time indicator line?

### Undo / Redo
- How do I undo or redo a change?

### Notifications
- When do I receive notifications?
- How do I mark a notification as read?
